VoxFrame

Speak it. VoxFrame handles the rest.

VoxFrame is a video editing app for content creators. It automatically trims a raw talking-head recording down to where you actually start and stop speaking, and generates on-screen captions from your speech. If you connect your YouTube account, VoxFrame uses the YouTube Data API to upload the finished video directly to your channel on your behalf — that's the only reason it requests access to your account, and it never reads, modifies, or deletes anything else on your channel.
