Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your VoxFrame account and its associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Email support@voxframe.org from the email address linked to your account (or with your Google/Apple sign-in email if you didn't use a password) with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm and process the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account identity — the email/password, Google, or Apple sign-in record is permanently removed from our authentication system (Firebase Authentication).
  • Locally stored data — OAuth tokens for connected platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok) and app preferences live only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes these immediately; we don't hold a server-side copy.

What isn't affected

  • Videos you've already published to YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok are not deleted — remove those directly on the respective platform.
  • Data already sent to third-party processing services (Groq, Google Gemini, Pexels) as part of editing a video is governed by those providers' own retention policies — see our Privacy Policy for what's sent to whom.

Questions

Reach us at support@voxframe.org for anything else related to your account or data.
