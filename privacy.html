Privacy Policy

Last updated:

VoxFrame ("we", "our", "the app") is a video-editing app that turns a spoken recording into a trimmed, captioned, ready-to-post clip. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Information the app processes

To do its job, VoxFrame processes the video and audio you choose to edit:

  • Video/audio content — the clip you record or select is trimmed, transcribed, and (optionally) has image overlays and captions added.
  • Account data for platforms you connect — if you link YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok to publish a video, we handle the OAuth access/refresh tokens and basic account info (e.g. channel name) needed to post on your behalf.

How your content is processed

Parts of the editing pipeline are handled by third-party AI services, which receive only what's needed to perform that step:

  • Groq (Whisper) — receives extracted audio to generate a speech transcript and word-level timing, used for auto-captions and trimming.
  • Google Gemini — receives the transcript (and, when image overlays are enabled, video frames) to identify words worth illustrating.
  • Pexels — receives search keywords (not your video) to find matching stock photos for overlays.

These services process this data under their own privacy policies. We don't operate our own servers to store or process your video — all editing currently happens on your device or via direct calls to the services above.

Publishing to YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok

If you choose to connect an account, VoxFrame uses that platform's official API to upload the video you export, using the title, description, and hashtags you provide. We only request the access needed to publish on your behalf — we do not read, modify, or delete your existing content on that platform.

Google API Services User Data Policy: VoxFrame's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. You can also revoke VoxFrame's access at any time from your Google, Meta, or TikTok account security settings.

How we protect your data

  • In transit: every call to Google, Meta, TikTok, Groq, Gemini, and Pexels is made over HTTPS/TLS. We never transmit tokens or video data over an unencrypted connection.
  • At rest: OAuth access/refresh tokens are stored in local, app-private storage that the Android operating system sandboxes from every other app on the device — no other app, and no VoxFrame server (we don't run one), can read them.
  • Access control: tokens are only ever read by the app to make an authenticated request on your behalf, immediately before an upload. They are never logged, and are removed the moment you disconnect an account or uninstall the app.
  • Minimal scope: we request only the OAuth permissions each platform requires to publish a video on your behalf (e.g. YouTube's youtube.upload scope) — never broader read/manage/delete access to your account.

Data we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your video content to train our own models.
  • We don't share your data with advertisers.

Children's privacy

VoxFrame is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

As VoxFrame adds features (such as user accounts or a premium tier), this policy will be updated to reflect them, with the "last updated" date above changed accordingly.
